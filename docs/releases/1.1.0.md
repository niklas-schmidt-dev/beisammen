Neu in 1.1.0:
• Benachrichtigungen zu neuen Circle-Mitgliedern und gezieltere Hinweise auf Reaktionen.

## App Store — English

```text
New in 1.1.0:
• Notifications for new Circle members and more relevant reaction alerts.
• Notifications open the right post or Circle, with improved unread activity badges.
• More reliable importing of iCloud photos, Live Photos and videos.
• Fixed an issue where characters could disappear while typing captions.
```
